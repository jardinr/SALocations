02 The Industry Workflow

The traditional location scouting and pitching process is well-structured, but it suffers from severe speed bottlenecks. The problem is not the process itself, it is how long each manual step takes.

Step Time Today Where the Problem Sits
Review brief & list locations ~20 min Fully manual read; depends heavily on researcher's familiarity
Match to previous sites 10–15 min Memory-dependent cross-referencing of un-tagged archives
Setup project in Pitch Decks 30–45 min Repetitive manual setup for every brief
Select/transfer from Archive 45–90 min Direct live dependency on Wi-Fi stability and messy folders
Buffer: connectivity issues 15–30 min Unpredictable delays
Total time per brief 2 – 3½ hrs Compounds heavily with volume. Extremely hard to scale.
